Sidekick and Notion AI, closer

Bigger sketches of the two you asked about, drawn from their help pages, changelogs and reviews (the sites themselves are blocked from this sandbox, so these are my drawings, not screenshots). Three states each, then the Notion face in its moods, then what each would look like on Lucy at real size.

Shopify Sidekick

A side panel inside the admin. Opened from a Sidekick button in the black top bar; also voice, and on desktop it can watch your screen and walk you through a task.

Worth copying from Sidekick
  • Starters in the business's own words, three of them, right above the input, not generic "Summarise text".
  • Approve before it happens: a proposal card with the fields visible and one primary button. Lucy already sets reminders and keeps ideas; showing the card first makes it feel safe.
  • Full-screen for long chats and a panel that keeps working after you close it.
  • Not for Lucy: the side-panel-in-the-admin layout (Lucy is a floating box), voice, screen share, Pulse on Home (the morning brief already does that job).

Notion AI

A face in the corner of every page. Drawn in-house (the brief: make Notion feel more like a game than a productivity app), then animated by the studio BUCK with only eyes, brows and a nose.

Worth copying from Notion
  • A face instead of a letter. Lucy's cream circle with three brown strokes could wake up while she thinks and close its eyes when she's done. One drawing, used everywhere she appears.
  • Verbs as rows on the empty state, each a thing Lucy actually does.
  • Sources as chips under an answer (Lucy already has a sources line; chips make it readable) and a small "Copy" under long answers.
  • Not for Lucy: the sources picker and model picker (one business, one model), the @ page mention, the keyboard-shortcut culture.
