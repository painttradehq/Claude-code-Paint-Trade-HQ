Every painting business keeps employee records somewhere. Usually that is a folder in the ute, a photo of a licence in the owner's phone, pay rates in the bookkeeper's head, and hours on a text message every Friday. It works, in the way that a wet-paint sign works, right up until somebody leans on it.

Paint Trade HQ gives each person one page, and connects that page to the crew app in their pocket.

## The Employees page

Each crew member has a profile: contact details, **documents** such as licences and IDs, **pay rates**, start and end dates, whether they have access to the crew app, and which job they are on. The page tells you when a document is missing, so you are not finding out at an audit.

**Pay rates** sit on the person: hourly, daily, and an overtime rate (leave it blank and it uses the hourly rate). There is one money rule everywhere in the app: regular hours times the hourly rate plus overtime hours times the overtime rate, or days times the daily rate for daily-rate people. The payroll file uses that rule, and so does the labour cost on every project page, so the two never disagree.

## Onboarding is a code or a QR

A new start does not need an account set up for them by hand. You add the person, and the page gives you an **onboarding code or QR**. They scan it on their phone, sign in to the crew app, and they are in: today's job with the address, the time clock, and whatever else you have switched on for the crew.

What the crew see is your decision, page by page, in **Settings › Crew app**: which pages are on and which notifications they get. The crew never see prices, margins, admin pages or the AI assistant, whatever you switch on.

## The time clock and the pay week

Crew **sign in and sign out** on their phones. The app records the GPS position at those two moments, the address, and the distance from the job site, and flags a sign-in outside the job's radius. It records nothing in between: no tracking through the day. **Operating hours** in Settings set your start and end time and working days; the crew get a reminder in the app to sign in before the start and out at the end, and an auto sign-out time closes a session somebody forgot.

<figure class="shot shot--phone"><div class="shot__frame"><img src="/screens/webp/39-crew-today-390.webp" alt="The crew app Today screen: the time clock, today's job with the address, and the inbox" width="780" height="1688" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></div><figcaption>The crew's Today screen: the time clock, today's job with its address, and the inbox.</figcaption></figure>

On Small business and up, the **Timesheets page** turns those sign-ins into the pay week: a grid per person, each day's sessions in a drawer ("General" for time with no job), overtime split by your thresholds (the defaults are 8 hours a day and 38 a week), lock the week, and export the **payroll CSV** with hours and, for anyone with pay access, the pay columns. Your bookkeeper gets a file, not a text message.

## Office staff are team admins, not crew

Not everyone who needs the admin side needs all of it. **Team admins** are office staff with access page by page: the person who does invoicing can have Invoices and the CRM and nothing else. The crew get the crew app only. Seats on each plan count admins and employees separately.

<div class="scenario"><span class="eyebrow">A realistic Monday</span>

Say a second-year apprentice starts with you on Monday. On Sunday night you add him on the Employees page, type his hourly rate, and leave the overtime rate blank so it follows the hourly rate. The page shows two documents missing: his licence and his white card. You send him the QR.

Monday, 6:50 am, he scans the QR in the car park, signs up in the crew app, and today's job is there with the address. He signs in on site; the app notes the time, the position and that he is inside the job's radius. He sends you a photo of his licence at smoko and you upload it to his page. Knock-off, he signs out. Tuesday he forgets; the auto sign-out closes it at the time you set and you fix the minutes in the drawer.

Friday afternoon you open Timesheets. His week is 39.5 hours, so 38 regular and 1.5 overtime by your thresholds. You lock the week and export the CSV to the bookkeeper. His first week is on the record the way it actually happened, and nobody sent a Friday text.</div>

## What it replaces

The folder, the licence photo in your camera roll, the Friday text with the hours, and the quiet argument about whether it was 38 hours or 40. The record now comes from the person signing in on site, costed by rates you set once, and kept on one page.

<p class="plan-note">The Employees page, pay rates, the crew app and the time clock are on every plan. The Timesheets page and the payroll CSV are on Small business and up. Team admins are counted as admin seats on each plan.</p>
