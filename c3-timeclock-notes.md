# C3 — Time clock: the job on sign-in, location from the phone, the off-site result shown to the crew, My hours (v2, not sent)

Prototype: the **Time clock card** on Today and the **My hours** screen in `c1-crew-shell.html` — `?screen=app&tab=today` (signed out), tap Sign in for the job sheet (`&picker=1` opens it directly), `?screen=app&tab=today&state=in` (signed in to today's job), `&state=in&offsite=1` (flagged by the geofence), `&state=in&nogps=1` (recorded without a position), `&state=in&job=general`, `&jobs=0` (no jobs: Sign in goes straight to General), `?screen=hours` (My hours, this pay week), `?screen=hours&state=in` (with the open session), `?screen=hours&week=prev` (a locked past week), `?screen=hours&hours=0` (empty week). Built from handover **§3** (the three `time-clock.tsx` rows), **§4.5–4.6** and **§7 C3**, the Privacy Policy sentences on location, and the code facts in §2 below (four parallel readers on 12 Oct: backend time clock, the two crew surfaces, location and privacy, hours and settings). v2 (12 Oct): Josh answered handover **§6.8 yes** — crew see their week total with the owner's rounding and overtime — so My hours is the pay week (§4) on a new crew-only route (§6). Status: **Josh said send on 12 Oct; goes after follow-up C.**

## 1. What C3 is, in one paragraph

The crew member taps **Sign in** on Today, picks the job from their own list (or **General**), the phone takes one position, and the sign-in is stored with the job's id — so the owner's geofence runs and the Timesheets off-site flag becomes real. The result comes back to the crew in the owner's words ("820 m off site"). Location is taken once at sign-in and once at sign-out, never in between, and the card says so. A crew member whose phone gives no position still signs in; the owner sees that sign-in as "No GPS". The phone no longer calls OpenStreetMap. **My hours** (Me › My hours) shows the crew member's own pay week with the same numbers the owner's Timesheets show: rounded sessions, regular and overtime, by day. The crew app has no AI.

## 4. What a crew member sees after C3

**Time clock card (Today)** — the only place to sign in or out.

Signed out: grey dot, "Signed out"; "Not on the clock"; one line **"Last signed out {h:mm am/pm} {today | yesterday | Sat 4 Oct} · {H} h {MM} m · {last_project}"** from status `last_sign_out`, the new `last_total_hours` and `last_project` (the hours part is dropped when `last_total_hours` is null, the job part when `last_project` is null; "No sign-ins yet" when `last_sign_out` is null); no job row; button **Sign in**; under the button, in muted 12.5 px, the one location line: **"Your location is recorded only when you sign in or out, never in between."** (always shown, both states; the handover §4.6 line, kept to that one fact — no retention promise).

Signed in: green dot, "Signed in"; **"{H} h {MM} m on the clock"** from `duration_hours`, ticking every minute on the phone; **"Since {h:mm am/pm} {today | yesterday | Sat 4 Oct}"** from `sign_in_time`; the job row **"Job: {project_name}"** with a chevron that opens `/employee/project/{project_id}` (status gains `project_id`), or **"Job: General"** with no chevron and no tap when `project_id` is null; then, when the session is flagged (`session.outside_radius` from status, or the sign-in response `warning`), the amber block **"{dist} off site"** / **"Further than {r} m from the job. Your hours still count; your employer sees the distance."** — `dist` = "{n} m" under 1000 m, "{x.x} km" from 1000 m (as the admin's `dist()`), `r` = `geofence_radius_m` (`warning.radius_m` right after sign-in); when the open session has no position (status `session.location` has no `latitude`), the grey line **"No location from this phone. Your employer sees this sign-in without a position."**; button **Sign out** (outlined); the location line.

Both: while a position is being taken, the button reads **"Getting your location…"** and is disabled (up to 10 s). Status still polls every 60 s while signed in, as built, so an admin fix or the auto sign-out flips the card within a minute.

**The job sheet** — tapping Sign in opens a bottom sheet **"Which job?"** over Today: rows in the Jobs-tab order, **Now jobs first** (`in_progress`, `on_hold`), then **Coming up** (`quoted`), each the Jobs-tab row without its meta line (name, pill, "{client} · {address}"); the last row **"General"** / "Not on a job today"; button **Cancel**; tapping the dimmed area also closes it. Done jobs are never listed. With no Now or Coming-up job at all, **no sheet**: Sign in goes straight to General.

**Sign-in, in order:** job picked → sheet closes → `getPosition()` (§5) → `POST /timeclock/sign-in` with `{project_id, project_name, location, notes: null}`; `project_name` is the row's `name` (the backend stores the name verbatim, §2, so it must travel), `location` is `{latitude, longitude, accuracy, address}` with **`address` = the picked job's `address` from the list** (null for General) — the phone never looks its own address up; General sends `project_id: null, project_name: "General"`. **Without a position** (permission denied, no fix within 10 s, or no location service): **`location: {address: "No GPS"}`** — no coordinates at all, **never 0,0**; the geofence does not run and the owner's drawer and Live tab print "No GPS" (their own wording for admin-added sessions). The card is rendered from the response (`warning` → the amber block) and from the next status poll.

**Sign-out:** no sheet; `getPosition()` → `POST /timeclock/sign-out {location, notes: null}` (same `location` rules; `address` = the open session's job address when known, else null); the card then shows the signed-out state with the response's `total_hours` and `project_name` until the next status poll (which carries the same numbers through `last_total_hours`).

**Errors:** a 400/404 `detail` from the backend ("Already signed in. Please sign out first.", "Not currently signed in.", "Project not found") is shown as one inline line under the button (`crew-clock-error`, the C1 login error style) and status is reloaded, which clears it on the next render; no network → "No connection. Try again." The current screen's alerts ("Location Required", "Success — Signed in successfully!", "Signed out! Total hours: {n}") go; nothing pops up.

**My hours** (`/employee/time-clock`, stack screen, no tab bar) — back **"Me"**, title **"My hours"**; one call, `GET /timeclock/my-hours?week_start=` (§6), the current pay week by default. Under the title a week row: **‹** (previous week; always enabled), centre **"Week of {Mon 6 Oct}"** with the small line **"This week · {Mon 6 Oct} – {Sun 12 Oct}"** (just "{start} – {end}" for a past week), **›** (next week; disabled on the current week); the arrows are 44 × 44. Then the week card: big **"{H} h {MM} m"** (`total`), line **"{regular} regular · {overtime} overtime"**, and on the right a pill **"Locked"** (grey) when `locked`, else **"Approved"** (green) when `approved`, else none. Then the days with sessions, in week order, each with a header **"{Mon 6 Oct}"** left and **"{hours}"** right, followed by **" · {overtime} overtime"** in amber when the day's overtime is above zero; rows: **"{in_label} – {out_label}"** (the live session: "{in_label} – now" with the green pill **"On the clock"**; a past session never signed out: "{in_label} – not signed out"), the amber pill **"{dist} off site"** when `off_site`, the sub-line **"{project_name | General}"** followed by " · Edited by {edited_by_name} · {edit_reason}" when `edited` and not `auto_closed`, " · {edit_reason}" when `auto_closed` (the stored "Closed automatically at 6:00 pm"), " · Added by {added_by_name}" plus " · {edit_reason}" when `added_by_admin`; the right column **"{H} h {MM} m"** from the session's rounded `hours`, absent for a session with no sign-out (it counts 0 in every total, as the owner's grid). Foot line: **"Rounded to the nearest {rounding} min. "** only when `thresholds.rounding` is above zero, then **"Overtime after {per_day} h a day or {per_week} h a week."** (whole numbers without decimals, else one decimal). Empty week: **"No hours this week yet."** on the current week, **"No hours this week."** on a past one. Hours "{H} h {MM} m" from decimal hours to the minute; dates from the route's `date` via the existing `fmtWeekday`; times are the route's labels (the business's time zone, the same as the owner's timesheet). No sign-in or sign-out on this screen; the old "Recent Activity" and the `my-records` call go.

**Me › My hours** row sub-line becomes "Your sign-ins and hours, by day".
