## The advice, verbatim

> First, we recommend only providing the Terms of Service policy with the amendments outlined below for the purposes of your testing. This is premised on the assumption that your app is free, data is not traded, and it is clearly advertised to users that it is used at their own risk, for example by providing the policy upon sign-up.
>
> Second, we recommend requiring users to click an 'I agree' button or sign something to that effect when providing the Terms of Service.
>
> Third, we suggest the following amendments to the Terms of Service:
>
> - We suggest removing section 8 (Fees after tester program)
> - Replace section 12 (warranties) and move to become section 1 with:
>   - Paint Trade HQ is currently free and is being tested by you at your own risk. Therefore, Paint Trade HQ is provided 'as is' and 'as available' without warranty of any kind, either express or implied. To the maximum extent permitted by applicable Australian law, the developer(s) disclaim all warranties, whether express, implied, statutory, or otherwise. Your use of the app is at your own sole risk. In no event shall the developers be liable for any direct, indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages arising out of or in connection with the use of or inability to use the app.
> - We suggest adding another section (anywhere) with the clear term:
>   - We do not sell, trade, or otherwise rent your personal data to third parties for commercial or marketing purposes. We may collect and retain your usage data and interaction history to maintain, protect, and improve our services, such as debugging system errors, analysing performance trends, and developing new features. Wherever feasible, data used for these internal optimization purposes is anonymized or aggregated. We reserve the right to disclose your personal information or data if we are strictly required to do so by law, court order, subpoena, or a valid legal request from law enforcement authorities. We will only disclose the specific data that is legally mandated, and we make every effort to verify the legitimacy of any such government or police request before releasing any information.
>
> Fourth, on trademark and copyright.
>
> - As discussed, AI-assisted coding and the impact on copyright claims is an emerging area of law. Given this situation, it is recommended as much evidence of authorship is kept as is reasonably possible and marking your app and associated policies with the copyright + legal entity name.
> - We recommend lodging a trademark with IP Australia when possible. It takes several months for the trademark to be processed, and given that copyright claim may be disputed, a competitor may replicate your app and use your logo.
> - IP clauses are a standard part of employment agreements, so including such a clause when you employ independent contractors is recommended.
>
> Finally, when you get to launch, if you would like us to provide legal advice our current rates are $220 (inc GST) per hour for a solicitor (i.e. me) and $350 (inc. GST) per hour for principal solicitor.

## What it means, in plain words

1. **Testing phase = the Terms only.** The Privacy Policy, the client-contract questions (pack 03), the employment-document questions (04), the data map (05) and the fees section are all deferred to launch, when they come back as paid work at the rates above.
2. **The Terms change in three places**: the fees section goes; the warranties section is replaced by the lawyer's "at your own risk" text and becomes section 1; a "we do not sell your data" section is added. Done in `../terms-of-service.md` (version 1.2).
3. **Show the Terms at sign-up and make people click "I agree".** The sign-up already has an unticked "I agree to the Terms…" box with a link; the advice is to *provide* the text there too, and to get the same click from the testers already signed up when the new version lands.
4. **Mark the app and the Terms with "© + legal entity name".**
5. **Josh's own to-dos** (outside the app): lodge the trade mark with IP Australia; put an IP / confidentiality clause in the contractor agreement (Adam); keep the evidence of authorship (the round-by-round logs, specs and prototypes in the two repositories already do this — keep going).
6. **Launch-phase gap to remember:** these Terms say the app is free. The moment anyone pays for a plan (Round 80 billing with live Stripe keys), the premise fails and the launch-phase policies (fees, Privacy Policy, subscription terms) are needed first.
