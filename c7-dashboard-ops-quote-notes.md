## 1. What a crew member sees

**Dashboard.** Date on the left, the weather chip on the right of the same line ("⛅ 21° · Partly cloudy", today's job's suburb — the Round D forecast the job card used to show, moved up; no chip when there is no forecast). Greeting. While on the clock, a green chip under the greeting: "On the clock · 2 h 35 m · Wattle St repaint" (tap = the panel). Then Today's job, Unread with the Inbox link, End of day. No clock card on the page any more.

**The edge time clock.** A 4 px line on the left edge, a fifth of the screen tall, vertically centred, with a small chevron; grey when signed out, green while signed in. Tap it, or swipe in from the left edge, and the panel slides in (86 % of the width, at most 340 px) with the rest dimmed: the same clock card as before (status, the big line, last sign-out or since-when, the job row, the off-site or no-position block, Sign in / Sign out, the location line), a **My hours** row with this week's total, and "Swipe left to close". Tap the dim, the ×, or swipe left to close. It is there on every screen once signed in (job page, quote, reports…), never on the sign-in or onboarding screens. The job picker and the distance question open above it exactly as before.

**Tabs.** Dashboard · Jobs · Operations · Learn · Me. The unread badge sits on the Dashboard tab. The Inbox is a page under Dashboard (the "Inbox" link next to Unread; a "‹ Dashboard" back link at the top); the Dashboard tab stays lit while it is open. Everything in the Inbox (the message-your-employer row, Mark all read, the rows) is unchanged.

**Operations.** One list: My hours ("This week 43 h 00 m · your sign-ins by day"), My equipment, Training. Their screens are unchanged; their back buttons read "Operations". Learn keeps Coaching and How I'm going. Me keeps the profile, My documents, Team events, Sign out.

**Job page.** "THE WORK · 4 areas · 8 lines" (with "· about 46.5 h planned" when the owner's switch is on), then the summary card, then each area as its own section (name · dims · hours) with its note card and its lines; a line with a note, a paint or photos opens in place (chevron) and shows them; photos open the lightbox with the owner's marks and the caption. Added work as before. Then one row: **Open the quote** — "The whole quote as your employer built it, without the prices". The Scope screen of Round E is gone; its deep link lands on the job page.

**The quote.** Back = the job name, title "Quote", meta "Q-0042 · the client's copy, without the prices". One paper card that is the client's PDF (`services/pdf.py`, the "Trade Services" layout) laid out for the phone, in the PDF's section order: the letterhead (business name in serif, ABN, address, phone, email; the big accent **QUOTE**, Quote #, Date issued, Valid until, the status pill); **About us** with the blurb and the stat tiles (only when the business typed them); **Prepared for** (the client's name and the site address — the client's phone and email never reach crew) and **Job details** (Site, Est. duration, Start); **WHAT WE WILL DO** — the area heading rows with dims and the client-facing area note in italics, the lines as DESCRIPTION · QTY · UNIT with the sub-line "Area · prep · 2 coats · {show-on-quote note}"; **MATERIALS SUPPLIED**; **OPTIONAL EXTRAS** (the unchosen ones, with the client's sentence); **PRODUCTS AND COLOURS** (product with its surface, colour, where); **TERMS & CONDITIONS** numbered; the thank-you line with the business's phone and email. Under the paper one muted line: "The client's copy also carries the prices, the total and the payment terms." Left out, exactly: the RATE and AMOUNT columns, the Materials/optional amounts, the paint amount line, the Totals band, Payment terms & bank details, the presentation pages. No photos, no hours, no internal notes here — those live on the job page.

## 2. What it means for the live app (to check before the brief)

- **Edge panel**: a component mounted in `app/employee/_layout.tsx` (so it sits over the tabs and the stack screens alike), shown only with a crew session and after onboarding; the clock card moves out of the dashboard screen into it unchanged (same ids, same picker and distance sheets); the handle is a 28 px touch target with the 4 px line; open on tap or on a pan from the left edge (`PanResponder`, or `react-native-gesture-handler` if it is already a dependency — check), close on the scrim, the × or a pan left. **One thing to decide**: on Android the left edge is also the system back gesture, so a swipe from the very edge can be taken by the OS; the tap always works. If it clashes on Josh's phone, the right edge (Samsung's side) is the one-line change.
- **Weather chip**: the dashboard already calls `GET /projects/mine/{id}/weather` (Round D) for the card line; render it in the header instead. Nothing new on the backend.
- **Tabs**: the Today route is already `/employee/dashboard` — the label changes. New `(tabs)/operations.tsx`; `(tabs)/inbox.tsx` leaves the bar (`href: null`) and gains the back link, or becomes `app/employee/inbox.tsx`; every `notifRoute` that pointed at the Inbox still works. The badge moves to the Dashboard tab.
- **Operations**: three rows to the existing routes. The hours sub needs this week's total — whatever the hours screen reads today (`routes/timeclock.py`'s crew week route) — check which route and whether a light call exists.
- **Job page**: the job screen renders the scope inline — read `GET /projects/mine/{id}/scope` next to the job call (or fold the scope into the job payload and drop `scope_detail`); `project/[id]/scope.tsx` redirects to the job page so old links live; the Lightbox stays shared.
- **The quote**: new `project/[id]/quote.tsx` and a new allow-list route `GET /projects/mine/{id}/quote` that walks the same renderers as `services/pdf.py`'s `render_estimate_pdf` in the business's saved section order (`_layout_sections`), returning `{business{name, abn, address, phone, email, logo_url}, number, issued, valid_until, status, about{blurb, stats}, client_name, site, duration, start, work[{area, dims, note_on_quote, lines[{name, sub, qty, unit}]}], materials[...], optional[...], products[{product, surface, colour, where}], terms[{title, text}], thanks}` — built from `flatten_estimate_lines` (the client rows: `name`, `notes`/sub, `qty`, `unit_label`, `area_name`, `area_dims`, `area_note`, `kind`, `optional`/`chosen`), `products_rows`, `parse_terms`, `about_parts`, `_valid_until`; never `rate`, `total`, `unit_price`, `line_total`, the totals, `paint_line`, the payment plan, the bank block, `clientPhone`, `clientEmail`; `_disclosures` (hazards) included when present. The test walks the payload for money keys as Round E's does. The phone layout drops the SURFACE column into the product's sub-line.
- **Removed**: the Scope stack screen (Round E) and its "Open the full scope" row. Keep Round E's tests on the route; retarget the screen tests.

## 3. Decisions for Josh (reverse any in one line)

1. **The on-the-clock chip** stays on the Dashboard while signed in (status, not a second card). Alternative: nothing on the Dashboard, the green edge line alone.
2. **The handle on the left** as asked. Alternative: the right edge (Samsung's side; no clash with Android's back gesture).
3. **The Inbox entry** = the existing Inbox link next to Unread, plus the unread badge on the Dashboard tab. Alternative: an envelope button in the header too.
4. **Learn keeps two rows** (Coaching, How I'm going). Alternative: fold them into Operations and drop the Learn tab (four tabs).
5. **The quote is the client's PDF**, section for section, minus the money (Josh, 28 Nov). Internal notes, marked photos and planned hours stay on the job page only.
6. **The client's name and site address** print on the paper (crew already see both on the job page); the client's email and phone never.
7. **Hazard disclosures** print when the quote has them (the client sees them); the prototype's sample quote has none.
8. **No tick-off** here (still E2).
