# C7 — Dashboard, the edge time clock, Operations, the scope on the job page, the quote as the client's PDF (v1, 28 Nov 2026 — the spec; checked against `the-newest-update-of-PTHQ@a71a8f3`)

Josh's ask (27 Nov, corrected 28 Nov): the time clock becomes a slide-in panel from the left edge (a thin line a fifth of the screen tall with a small arrow, Samsung edge-panel style); the weather sits top right next to the date; the Today tab becomes **Dashboard**; the Inbox tab becomes **Operations**; Operations holds **My hours**, **My equipment** (from Me) and **Training** (from Learn); on the job page the summary of the work is replaced by what "Open the full scope" showed; and that row now opens **the quote exactly as the client sees it — the PDF — without the prices**.

Prototype: `c1-crew-shell.html` (226 KB, replaces the Round E revision; 558 checks in `tools/test_c1.js` at 390 × 844 then 360 × 800). Deep links: `?screen=app&tab=today` (the Dashboard; `&state=in` on the clock), `&edge=1` (the panel open), `?screen=app&tab=ops`, `?screen=inbox`, `?screen=job&id=j1` (the scope inline; `&hours=1`, `&summary=0`, `&photos=0`), `?screen=quote&id=j1` (the quote), `?screen=quote&id=j2` (a bare quote, status sent, no About us, no terms). The prototype's clock, picker and distance sheets are the C3/D ones unchanged.

## 1. What a crew member sees

**Dashboard** (`/employee/dashboard`, the first tab, label "Dashboard", icon `grid-outline`). The date line on the left and, on the same line to the right, the weather chip for today's job's suburb — "⛅ 21° · Partly cloudy" (the Round D forecast the job card used to carry; no chip when there is no forecast or no job). The greeting. While on the clock, a green chip under the greeting: "On the clock · 2 h 35 m · Wattle St repaint" (tap = the panel). Then the alerts card as today, Today's job (without the weather line), Unread with the Inbox link, End of day. **No clock card on the page.** The unread badge sits on the Dashboard tab.

**The edge time clock.** A 4 px line on the left edge, 20 % of the screen tall, vertically centred, with a small chevron beside it; grey when signed out, green while signed in. Tap it, or swipe in from the left edge, and the panel slides in over everything (86 % of the width, at most 340 px; the rest dimmed): the heading "TIME CLOCK" with a × on the right, **the same clock card as today** (status dot and word, the big line, last sign-out or since-when, the job row, the off-site or no-position block, Sign in / Sign out, the error line, the location line), a **My hours** row ("This week 43 h 00 m · by day") and "Swipe left to close". Tap the dim, the ×, or swipe left to close; a swipe is never a tap. The panel is there on every crew screen once signed in and onboarded (tabs and stack screens alike), never on the sign-in, onboarding, reset or PIN screens. The job picker and the distance question open above the panel exactly as they open above the card today; after a sign-in the panel stays open showing the result.

**Tabs.** Dashboard · Jobs · Operations · Learn · Me. The Inbox is a page under Dashboard: the "Inbox" link next to Unread opens it; it has a "‹ Dashboard" back link at the top; while it is open the Dashboard tab stays lit. Everything in the Inbox (Message your employer, Mark all read, the rows, the tap routes) is unchanged; every notification link to `/employee/inbox` still lands there.

**Operations** (`/employee/operations`, icon `briefcase-outline`). One list: **My hours** ("This week 43 h 00 m · your sign-ins by day" — this week's total from the same route as the hours screen; "Your sign-ins and hours, by day" until it loads), **My equipment** ("Your gear, and requests for more"), **Training** ("Videos, required ones first, and your progress"). The three screens are unchanged; their back buttons read "Operations". Learn keeps Coaching and How I'm going. Me keeps the profile, My documents, Team events, Phone alerts, PIN sign-in, Sign out. The owner's switches still apply: `features.equipment` off hides the equipment row; `features.learn` off hides the Training row and the Learn tab as today.

**Job page.** "THE WORK · 4 areas · 8 lines" (plus "· about 46.5 h planned" when the owner's planned-hours switch is on), then the summary card ("What was quoted"), then each area as its own section (name · dims · hours) with its note card and its lines; a line with a note, a paint or photos opens in place (chevron) and shows them; photos open the lightbox with the owner's marks and the caption "{line} · {photo name} — {note}" (when the photo's name already contains the line's name the caption is the name alone). Added work as before. Then one row: **Open the quote** — "The whole quote as your employer built it, without the prices" — only when the job has a quote. Round E's Scope screen goes; its link redirects to the job page.

**The quote** (`/employee/project/{id}/quote`). Back = the job name, title "Quote", meta "Q-0042 · the client's copy, without the prices". One paper card = the client's PDF (`services/pdf.py`, the "Trade Services" layout) laid out for the phone, in the business's own section order (Settings → PDF & Brand Design, `_layout_sections`): the **letterhead** (the logo when there is one, the business name in serif, ABN, address, phone, email; on the right the big accent **QUOTE** — "REVISED QUOTE" when the quote was revised — Quote #, Date issued, Valid until, the status pill); **About us** with the blurb and up to four stat tiles (only when the business typed them); **Prepared for** (the client's name and the site address — the client's phone and email never reach crew) and **Job details** (Site, Est. duration, Start — only the ones set); **WHAT WE WILL DO** — the area heading rows with dims and the client-facing area note in italics, then the lines as DESCRIPTION · QTY · UNIT with the sub-line the client gets ("Living room · prep · 2 coats · {show-on-quote note}"); **MATERIALS SUPPLIED** (the material rows); **OPTIONAL EXTRAS** (the unchosen optional rows, with the client's sentence "Not included in the total. Tick any you would like on the online quote, or tell us when you accept."); **PRODUCTS AND COLOURS** (product with its surface under it, colour or "To be confirmed", where); **Hazard disclosures** when the quote has them (name, mode, where, text, the licence number when it applies); **TERMS & CONDITIONS** numbered ("1. Title" + text); the thank-you line ("Thank you for the opportunity to quote." or the business's own footer text) with "Questions? Call {phone} or email {email}." Under the paper one muted line: "The client's copy also carries the prices, the total and the payment terms." Left out, exactly: the RATE and AMOUNT columns everywhere, the Totals band, the paint amount line, Payment terms & details and the bank block, the presentation pages. No photos, no planned hours, no internal notes on the quote — those live on the job page.

## 5. Backend

1. **`GET /api/projects/mine/{project_id}/quote`** (`routes/projects.py`, registered next to `/scope`, above `GET /projects/mine/{project_id}`): `get_current_employee`; `_crew_project_or_404`; `est = db.estimates.find_one({_id: p.estimate_id, owner_admin_id})` or 404 `"This job has no quote."` (also when the project has no `estimate_id`); `bp = await _business_profile_for(aid)` (import from `routes.pdf`); `pm = await products_map(db, aid)`; `rows = flatten_estimate_lines(est, pm)` (after `migrate_estimate` once and `site_setup_on_read`, as `_crew_scope` does); `sections = [s["id"] for s in _layout_sections(bp, "estimate") if s["enabled"] and s["id"] not in ("total", "payment-bank", "presentation", "products")]` (`products` is the legacy cards block, empty on v2 quotes). Build, field by field:
   - `job_name`: `p.name`.
   - `business`: `{name: bp.business_name, abn, address, phone, email, logo_url}` (empty strings allowed), `accent: bp.pdf_accent_color`.
