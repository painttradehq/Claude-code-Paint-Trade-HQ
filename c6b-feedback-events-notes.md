# C6b — My feedback and Team events: the two crew screens that are broken today, rebuilt on crew-safe routes (v1, 1 Nov 2026, not sent)

Prototype: `#s-feedback` (`crew-feedback`, from Inbox › "Message {first}" `crew-inbox-message`; `?screen=feedback`, `&feedback=0` empty) and `#s-events` (`crew-events`, from Me › Team events `crew-me-events`; `?screen=events`, `&events=0` empty, `&birthdays=0`) in `c1-crew-shell.html`. Checks: 14 of the 524 in `tools/test_c1.js` (section "C6b"; `shots-f/c6b-*.png`, 390 × 844 and 360 × 800). Built from four readers over the clone and one verifier (1 Nov; 60 claims confirmed, 6 refuted and corrected here). Rides with it: the two Round C6 carry-overs (§4). Order: after follow-up E.

## 1. What C6b is, in one paragraph

Both screens exist in the crew app and neither works. **My feedback** reads an admin-only route, so every crew member sees "No feedback submitted yet" forever and never a reply; posting works but the owner's reply never reaches the phone. **Team events** calls the admin calendar route and gets every event of the business — meetings, billing reminders, client and project ids, every colleague's RSVP ids — then shows an intro hard-coded to "Josh Sano Painting & Decorating", four made-up event types, birthdays from an admin-only route (always empty), and an RSVP that cannot be undone. C6b gives each a crew route that returns only what the crew member may see: their own messages with the owner's replies, and the events the owner shared with the crew or invited them to, with birthdays as first name and day. Replies arrive in the Inbox. RSVP toggles. No AI, no pop-ups, no new permission.

## 2. What exists today (the clone at `dae9a2f`; file:line from the readers, verified)

**Feedback — two unrelated systems share the name.** `routes/feedback.py` is the Round 17b tester helper and platform inbox (`/api/feedback/threads`, `/unread`, `/api/platform/feedback/*`, PARKING_LOT.md, the "Josh replied" email; its gate 403s every crew token) — **C6b never touches it**. The crew system lives in `server.py` on `api_router` (`:803–901`, collection `db.feedback`): `POST /feedback {employee_id, feedback_text}` (any role; an employee must name themselves → 403 "Employees can only submit feedback for themselves"; stores `{_id, owner_admin_id, employee_id, feedback_text, submitted_at}`; sets `employees.feedback_submitted: true`; **no strip, no empty check, no length cap, no rate limit, no notification to the owner**; returns the raw row); `GET /feedback` (admin: the owner's CrewMessages list); `GET /feedback/employee/{employee_id}` (**admin-only** — the crew screen's read, hence 403 and "No feedback submitted yet"); `PUT /feedback/{feedback_id}/reply {admin_reply}` (admin; **one overwritten slot** `admin_reply` + `replied_at`; no `replied_by`; **writes no notification**); `DELETE /feedback/{feedback_id}` (admin). `test_r44_hygiene` and `test_tenant_isolation` pin the admin routes as they are. Timestamps are naive UTC ISO strings (`_utcnow`, `server.py:18–19`).

**Events** (`server.py:357–394, 647–725, 775`): `GET /events` and `GET /events/{id}` answer **any role** with the whole document — `customer_id`, `project_id`, `selected_employees`, `rsvp_list` (every attendee's employee id), `blocks_availability`, `owner_admin_id` — the leak. Categories `meetings | reminder | project | billing | birthday` (the drawer defaults to meetings); **no share-with-crew flag exists**; `recurrence` / `recurrence_until` on a single row; all-day events return `starts_at`/`ends_at` **null** from `_event_iso_aliases` (`:697–710`). `POST /events` (admin) writes an `event` notification with the **full description** in the message to the invitees, or to **all employees** when none are named, with **no `related_id` and no `link`** (`:672–673`); `PUT`/`DELETE` write none. `POST /events/{id}/rsvp/{employee_id}` ignores its body, has **no un-RSVP**, no deleted filter (`:775`); `rsvp_list` holds id **strings** (`ai_tools.py:609` expects dicts — a dead path since Round 34; leave the shape). **Birthdays:** `employees.dob`; `run_birthday_check` (`server.py:97`, scheduled in `worker.py:62`) notifies **every active employee of every business** about a birthday in any business — a live cross-tenant privacy defect outside both screens (§10); the crew screen's birthdays come from `GET /employees` (admin-only → empty). `employees.status` is never written; the owner's bridge row sits in `employees` (`admin_id` set; `routes/employees.py:140` excludes it); deleted rows carry `deleted: true`.

**Frontend (pre-C1 clone):** `app/employee/feedback.tsx` reads `/feedback/employee/{id}`, posts `{employee_id, feedback_text}`, `Alert.alert` for every outcome (a no-op on web), no test ids; `app/employee/team-events.tsx` hard-codes "At Josh Sano Painting & Decorating…" and four types, calls `GET /events` and `GET /employees`, RSVPs through the legacy route, no un-RSVP, `Alert.alert`. **After C1/C5/C6:** the feedback entry is the **Inbox** row "Message {first}" (`crew-inbox-message` → `/employee/feedback`), not a Me row; Team events is the Me row `crew-me-events` (→ `/employee/team-events`), hidden by `features.events`, and `_layout.tsx`'s HIDDEN list has `['events', /^\/employee\/team-events/]`; **`features.feedback` is wired nowhere** (no HIDDEN entry; the Inbox row shows regardless); the C1 Inbox tap rule opens a notification's `link`; the icon rule has no `event`/`birthday` case (bells). The Inbox endpoint returns whole notification documents.

## 3. What a crew member sees after C6b

**My feedback** (`/employee/feedback`, stack; from the Inbox row): back **"Inbox"**, title **"My feedback"**, meta **"Messages to {owner first name} and the replies. Only you and your employer see them."** Section **MESSAGE {OWNER FIRST NAME}**: a card with a three-line text box (placeholder "e.g. The van's rear tyre is getting low") and **Send**. Send with nothing → the box outlined red and **"Type your message first."** (cleared on typing); Send → `POST /feedback` → the message tops the list as "Sent just now", the box clears; a failure → the line under the box with the `detail` or **"No connection. Try again."**. Section **YOUR MESSAGES**: rows newest first — the text, **"Sent {Tue 28 Oct}"**, then either a tinted reply card **"Reply from {first} · {Wed 29 Oct}"** + the reply text, or the grey line **"No reply yet."**. Empty: **"Nothing sent yet. Your messages and the replies land here."** No status words, no threads (one reply per message, as the owner's screen works).

**Team events** (`/employee/team-events`, stack; from the Me row): back **"Me"**, title **"Team events"**, meta **"{n} coming up"** / **"Nothing coming up"**. Section **BIRTHDAYS** (only when there is at least one): chips **"🎂 {First} · {14 Nov}"**, the crew member's own as **"{First} (you) · {date}"**, the next three by date. Section **COMING UP**: one card per event — title with the pill **Invited** when the owner named them; **"{Fri 7 Nov · 7:00 – 7:30 am}"** + " · {location}" (an all-day event: **"{Sat 13 Dec}"**; a multi-day one: **"{Mon 22 Dec – Fri 9 Jan}"**); the owner's note; a foot with **"{n} going"** / **"1 going"** / **"No one has said yet"** and the toggle **"I'm going"** (outlined) ↔ **"I'm going ✓"** (filled) → `POST /events/{id}/rsvp {going}`; tapping the filled button un-RSVPs. A multi-day all-day event (a shutdown, a notice) has no foot. Empty: **"No team events coming up. When your employer shares one, it lands here."** No intro card, no event types, no colleague names, no RSVP lists.

**Inbox**: a `feedback_reply` notification ("Reply from {first}") opens My feedback; an `event` notification opens Team events; both show the calendar icon (birthday kinds too). Nothing else on the Inbox changes.

**Switches:** `features.feedback` false → the Inbox row "Message {first}" is gone and `/employee/feedback` lands on Today; `features.events` false → as C5 (the Me row and `/employee/team-events`). With the Inbox tab off, feedback is unreachable too (§8.6).
