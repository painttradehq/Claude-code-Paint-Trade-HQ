## 1. What C4 is, in one paragraph

A painter opens **Daily report** from Today or from the job page, and the form already knows the job (the one they clocked on to) and their hours today. They write what was done, add photos (which also land on the job's photo page), say whether there was a problem on site, and send. The owner's Daily reports page gets a report linked to the job with hours, a Flagged mark when there was a problem, and the photos. When the owner replies, the crew member's Inbox notification opens the report with the reply under it, and the list shows **Replied**. Edits are allowed for 24 hours, as today. No AI anywhere.
