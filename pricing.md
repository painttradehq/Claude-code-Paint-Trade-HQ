# Paint Trade HQ — pricing (decided by Josh, 11 October 2026)

All prices AUD per month. Dashboard is free for everyone. This is the decision of record; nothing in the app changes until a billing round is asked for.

## Josh's text, verbatim

> Paint Trade HQ — pricing. All prices AUD per month. Dashboard is free for everyone.
>
> Starter — 59. One admin plus three employees. Estimating and quoting with the full library, payments, invoicing, CRM, calendar, employee page, and project management, both the live project page and project details page.
>
> Middle — 179. Two admins plus five employees. Everything in Starter, plus the Presentation feature, auto-invoicing, the AI assistant, and SMS. Also unlocks these parts that are off in Starter: the business performance stats on the live project page, the AI summary on the project details page, timesheets, and the per-client AI summary in the CRM. Plus one hour of training for one person.
>
> Pro — 249. Three admins plus fifteen active employees. Everything in middle, plus the performance page, training videos, internal HR contracts with e-sign, and daily reports and equipment. Plus one hour of training per person, and free data migration from their old software, which lower tiers can buy as a paid add-on.
>
> Add-on seats. Extra admin: 25 on Starter and middle, 42 on Pro. Extra employee: 15 on Starter and middle, 25 on Pro.
