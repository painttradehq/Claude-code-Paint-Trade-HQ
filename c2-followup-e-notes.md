## 1. What this adds, in one paragraph

C2 already gives the assigned crew the quote's skeleton on the job page: areas with their measurements, every sold line with quantity, unit and the "prep · 2 coats" sub-line, the product and colour, one photo per line, approved added work, paints, crew-visible files, site contact and access notes, never a price. E adds the detail layer underneath, on its own screen so the job page stays short: the quote's scope summary ("What was quoted"), every area note (not only the ones ticked for the client), and for each line the estimator's note, the paint, and **all** the quote's photos for that area and line, exactly as the owner marked them up (the marks are in the image). Planned hours per area and line show only when the owner turns a new switch on in Settings › Crew app ("Show planned hours to crew", off by default). Optional lines the client did not accept stay out; prices, totals, margins and the client's details never appear. E2, later: tick lines off, progress per area for the owner, the daily report's "What was done" prefilled.

## 3. What a crew member sees

**Job page** (C2, one row added): under "The work", after the areas, the row **"Open the full scope"** with the sub-line **"{n} line{s} · the quote's notes and marked photos"** (`crew-job-scope`; n = the rows across `areas[].lines` after the optional-unchosen drop, added-work lines not counted) → `/employee/project/{id}/scope`. The row appears only when the job payload's `scope_detail` is true (§5: a summary, an area note, a line note, or a photo); otherwise nothing is added.

**Scope** (`/employee/project/{id}/scope`, stack): back = the job name, title **"Scope"**, meta **"{a} area{s} · {n} line{s}"** + **" · about {h} h planned"** when the payload carries `hours`. Then:
- **What was quoted** (a section label in the crew section-label style — uppercase by `textTransform`, as every section label on the screen; the dims/hours suffix on an area label is not transformed) and a note card with `summary` — only when the quote has one.
- One section per area in the quote's order: the label **"{area}"** + " · {dims}" when measured + " · {h} h" when the payload carries `hours` and the area's is greater than 0; the area's note as a note card when there is one (every area note, whether or not marked show-on-quote); the area's own photos (`area_photos`) as a grid under the note; then the lines as rows: title **"{name}"** + " · {qty} {unit}" when qty is set, **formatted exactly as C2's job page formats the same line** (c2-jobs-notes §3; that rule wins over the prototype's "Doors · × 4"), sub = C2's `sub` **with the trailing ` · {note}` removed** (the note shows once, below) + " · {h} h" when `hours` is carried and greater than 0. A line with any detail behind it (note, paint or photos) has a chevron and expands on tap (`aria-expanded`); a line with none has no chevron and does not open. Expanded: the estimator's **note**; the fact line **Paint {brand name · colour}** with only the word Paint in semibold text-primary and the value in text-secondary; the photo grid (3 across, C4's photo tile) showing each photo's `url` — the owner's marks are in the image; the tile's text and `accessibilityLabel` = the photo's `name`, else the line name. - **Added work** at the end: C2's approved added-work rows with the "approved {date}" pill and the row's `timeframe` text after the sub-line when there is one. No hours (variations have none).
- **No detail at all** (a deep link or Back after the owner stripped the detail): the skeleton still renders — the meta, the areas and their lines, no summary, no chevrons — never a blank screen.
- **`features.photos` false**: the photo grids are omitted everywhere on Scope, as the job page already hides its Photos section; a line whose only detail is photos then has no chevron.
- Nothing else: no prices, totals, discounts, margins, options the client declined, client email or phone, the quote's reference or payment terms; no tick boxes (E2); job-level photos (no area, no line) stay off Scope in E.
