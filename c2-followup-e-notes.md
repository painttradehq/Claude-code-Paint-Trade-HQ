## 1. What this adds, in one paragraph

C2 already gives the assigned crew the quote's skeleton on the job page: areas with their measurements, every sold line with quantity, unit and the "prep · 2 coats" sub-line, the product and colour, one photo per line, approved added work, paints, crew-visible files, site contact and access notes, never a price. E adds the detail layer underneath, on its own screen so the job page stays short: the quote's scope summary ("What was quoted"), every area note (not only the ones ticked for the client), and for each line the estimator's note, the size and condition, the paint, and **all** the quote's photos for that area and line with the owner's marks drawn on them, tappable to full screen. Planned hours per area and line show only when the owner turns a new switch on in Settings › Crew app ("Show planned hours to crew", off by default). Optional lines the client did not accept stay out; prices, totals, margins and the client's details never appear. E2, later: tick lines off, progress per area for the owner, the daily report's "What was done" prefilled.

## 3. What the crew member sees

**Job page** (C2, one row added): under "The work", after the areas, a row **"Open the full scope"** with the sub-line **"{n} lines · the quote's notes and marked photos"** (`crew-job-scope`) → `/employee/project/{id}/scope`. The row appears only when the scope route has anything beyond C2's skeleton (a summary, an area note, a line note, size, condition or a photo); otherwise nothing is added.

**Scope** (`/employee/project/{id}/scope`, stack): back = the job name, title **"Scope"**, meta **"{a} area{s} · {n} lines"** + **" · about {h} h planned"** when the switch is on and the quote has hours. Then:
- **WHAT WAS QUOTED** (section label) and a note card with `scope_summary` — only when the quote has one.
- One section per area in the quote's order: the label **"{area}"** + " · {dims}" when measured + " · {h} h" when the switch is on (dims and hours not uppercased); the area's note as a note card when there is one (every area note, not only show-on-quote); then the lines as rows: title **"{name}"** + " · {qty unit}" when there is a quantity, sub = the C2 `sub` + " · {h} h" when the switch is on. A line with any detail behind it (note, size, condition, paint or photos) has a chevron and expands on tap (`aria-expanded`); a line with none has no chevron and does not open. Expanded: the estimator's **note**; **Size {size} · Condition {condition}** (either, both, or the line absent); **Paint {brand product · colour}**; the photo grid (3 across, the C2 photo tile) with the owner's marks drawn over each thumbnail; tap a photo → full-screen lightbox (C6's `components/crew/Lightbox.tsx`, extended to draw the same marks) with the caption "{line} · {photo note}", close with × or a tap outside.
- **Added work** at the end: C2's approved added-work rows with the "approved {date}" pill, + " · {h} h" when the switch is on.
- Nothing else: no prices, totals, discounts, margins, options the client declined, client email or phone, the quote's reference or payment terms; no tick boxes (E2).
