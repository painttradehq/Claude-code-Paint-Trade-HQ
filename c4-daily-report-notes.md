# C4 — Daily report: the crew form rebuilt, opened from the job, photos on the job, the owner's reply shown (v1, not sent)

Prototype: three stack screens in `c1-crew-shell.html` — the list `?screen=reports` (`&reports=0` empty), the form `?screen=report-new&project=j1` (`&project=j2` a coming-up job, `&jobs=0` no jobs → General), one report `?screen=report&id=r1` (replied) and `?screen=report&id=r2` (flagged, edited, no reply). Also reachable in the shell: Today › End of day row → the list; the job page's **Daily report** button → the form with that job; the feedback notification on Today → that report. Built from handover **§4.1** and **§7 C4**, the admin daily-reports spec (`memory/specs/daily-reports/daily-reports-notes.md`, Round 19) and the code facts in §2 (two readers on 14 Oct: the crew form and the backend; the admin drawer, photo merge and limits read directly). Status: **waiting for Josh's review; nothing sent.**

## 1. What C4 is, in one paragraph

A painter opens **Daily report** from Today or from the job page, and the form already knows the job (the one they clocked on to) and their hours today. They write what was done, add photos (which also land on the job's photo page), say whether there was a problem on site, and send. The owner's Daily reports page gets a report linked to the job with hours, a Flagged mark when there was a problem, and the photos. When the owner replies, the crew member's Inbox notification opens the report with the reply under it, and the list shows **Replied**. Edits are allowed for 24 hours, as today. No AI anywhere.
