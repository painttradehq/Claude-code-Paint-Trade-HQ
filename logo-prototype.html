Paint Trade HQ · logo prototype

A double-P mark in the spirit of PayPal's construction — two offset letterforms, the overlap as a third tone — made ours with the app's purple and a painter's touch. Three directions to pick from.

1 · The mark, three directions

A · Double P
Two offset P's, back one in the light purple, front in the deep purple. The overlap reads darker, like PayPal's third tone.
B · Double P with a drip
Same mark, one paint drip off the stem. Says "paint" without a brush cliché.
C · Roller P
The bowl of the P is a roller sleeve, the stem its handle, doubled the same way. The most "trade" of the three.
