## 1. What C5 is, in one paragraph

A new starter scans the owner's invite, and the phone checks the link first: a dead link says so on one screen instead of after five steps. The welcome screen carries the owner's video (a YouTube link the owner sets) and then five short steps in the brand: who you are (15 or older, a line for 15–17 about the guardian's consent), a photo, your documents (photo or PDF, the two required ones with their expiry), the owner's own "Our team" page (title, intro and sections the owner writes once in Settings — Josh's current text becomes his business's content), and one agreement screen that says in three lines what the employer sees, that location is taken only at sign-in and sign-out, and that documents are never shared by link — with the real Terms of Service and Privacy Policy linked and a tick box the backend records (version, time, IP), the way the owner's sign-up already does. The hard-coded "employment agreement" step goes: an employment agreement is a document the owner issues through Contracts. **A crew member the owner adds by temporary password goes through the same wizard at first sign-in** (details to confirm, the temporary password and a real one, photo, documents, agree) — today they can never change that password, because no route exists; C5 adds the route and a Change password row on Profile. **Profile** becomes the crew member's own page: photo, details, address, and the same eight documents with their status and expiry, each replaceable without wiping the others (a bug today). **My documents** (contracts, Round 22) keeps every call and rule and gets the crew look, inline lines instead of pop-ups, and on the phone a real share sheet for the signed PDF instead of "it was emailed to you" (nothing is ever attached to that email). Four backend fixes come with it, all in the crew's own routes: the sign-in response and the crew's own record stop carrying the owner's private notes, pay rates and unshared coaching notes; the register route checks age, password and file type and size; the invite is checked before the email, so the public register route no longer answers "this email already exists" to anyone. No AI anywhere.
