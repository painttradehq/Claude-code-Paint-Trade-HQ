## 1. What this follow-up is, in one paragraph

The owner's Crew app settings (Round 79) now hold a sign-in distance and a rule. This follow-up makes the crew app obey the rule, at sign-in **and** at sign-out. **Flag** is what C3 built: the sign-in is recorded, the crew sees the amber "820 m off site" block, the owner sees the flag. **Ask** is what Josh chose on 18 Oct: a crew member further than the distance from the job's pin is asked **"You're 820 m from the job — Sign in anyway?"** before anything is written; the same question at sign-out; a phone with no position is asked **"We can't find your location — Sign in anyway?"**; what they confirm is recorded as confirmed and the owner sees it with the distance. **Block** is what Round 79 built: a sign-in further than the distance with an accurate fix is refused; without a position, or with a poor fix, it is recorded and flagged; a sign-out is never refused. The question is the one thing Round 79 does not have: **Ask** is a third rule value this follow-up adds, and the owner's page needs one more radio for it (§7). Two small things ride along from the 21 Oct parking lot: Today's job card shows the weather for the job's suburb (the same Open-Meteo reading the owner's home strip uses, cached together), and a new daily report starts with the weather chips already ticked from it. No new permission, no new provider, nothing in the background; location is still taken once at sign-in and once at sign-out. The crew app has no AI.

## 4. What a crew member sees after this follow-up

**Sign in, rule Ask, further than the distance.** Job picked → "Getting your location…" (as C3) → the position goes to the backend → the backend answers "ask" (§6) → a sheet over Today, in the job-sheet style: title **"You're {dist} from the job"** (`dist` = "{n} m" under 1000 m, "{x.x} km" from 1000 m, from `distance_m`), one line **"Your employer sees the distance if you sign in from here."**, **Sign in anyway** (primary, 54 px) and **Cancel** (outlined, 10 px below). Behind it the card is unchanged (Signed out, Sign in). **Cancel** closes the sheet; nothing was recorded. **Sign in anyway** sends the same sign-in again with `confirmed: true` (the position is not taken again) → the signed-in card with the amber block exactly as C3 ("820 m off site" / "Further than 50 m from the job. Your hours still count; your employer sees the distance.").

**Sign out, rule Ask, further than the distance.** Sign out → "Getting your location…" → the backend answers "ask" → the same sheet with **"Your employer sees the distance if you sign out from here."** and **Sign out anyway** / **Cancel**. Cancel: still on the clock, the card unchanged. Sign out anyway: the signed-out card as C3 ("Last signed out 9:39 am today · 2 h 35 m · Wattle St repaint"); the crew is not shown the sign-out distance (the owner is).

**No position, rule Ask** (permission denied, no fix within 10 s, no location service), at sign-in or sign-out: the sheet **"We can't find your location"** / **"Check that location is on for this app. Your employer sees this sign-in without a position if you go on."** ("sign-out" at sign-out), **Sign in anyway** (or **Sign out anyway**) / **Cancel**. The phone asks this one itself (it knows the rule from `GET /employee/app-settings`, §5; no round trip). Anyway → recorded with `{address: "No GPS"}` and `confirmed: true`; the card shows C3's grey line after a sign-in.

**Rule Block (Round 79 as built).** A sign-in further than the distance with an accurate fix (the backend's 409): the sheet **"You're {dist} from the job"** / **"Your employer only allows sign-ins within {r} m of the job. Get closer and try again."** with one button **OK** (outlined; no "anyway"); OK closes it, nothing recorded. A far sign-in with a poor fix (`accuracy_m` over 25) is recorded and flagged — the amber block, as Flag. A sign-in without a position is recorded — the grey line, as C3. **A sign-out is never refused**: it is recorded and flagged for the owner, and the crew sees the signed-out card as C3, no question.

**Rule Flag.** Exactly C3: no sheet; a far sign-in is recorded and flagged (the amber block); a no-position sign-in is recorded (the grey line); a sign-out stores its position and is now also measured, silently.

**General.** No job, no pin: nothing to measure, no distance question. A General sign-in or sign-out **without a position** is asked under Ask (a position is recorded for General too) and recorded under Block and Flag.

**Errors.** A 409 is the question (above). Any other failure of the second call (network, 400 "Already signed in…") shows as C3's inline line under the button; the sheet is already closed.

**Today's job card — the weather line.** Under the name/address/pill row and above "{names} with you": **"{emoji} {temp}° · {condition} · {rain}% rain"** (e.g. "⛅ 21° · Partly cloudy · 10% rain"; `temp` rounded to a whole number; the emoji is the backend's, 16 px, aligned like the row icons). Only on Today's job card, only when the route (§6) returns a reading; while it loads, or when it returns null (no pin, no suburb, Open-Meteo down), the line is simply absent — no placeholder, no error. The card stays one tap target (Open job).

**Daily report — chips preselected.** When the form opens for a **new** report with a job (the clock's job, `?project=`, or the first in-progress job), the weather chips start ticked from the same reading: rain / drizzle / shower / thunder / snow in the condition → **Rainy**, else cloud / overcast / fog → **Cloudy**, else **Sunny**; plus **Windy** when the day's max wind is 30 km/h or more; plus **Hot** when the day's max is 30° or more, or **Cold** when it is 12° or less. General, no reading, or no network → nothing ticked. The crew tap chips on and off as before; changing the job in the form does not change the chips; **Edit** keeps the stored chips (never the forecast). Nothing else on the form changes.
